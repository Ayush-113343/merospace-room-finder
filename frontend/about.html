Our Mission

Make renting transparent, fast, and fair

Nepal's rental market has long been dominated by word-of-mouth and unregulated brokers. Tenants overpay. Landlords struggle to find reliable tenants. Both sides waste enormous time.

Our mission is simple — give both landlords and tenants the tools they need to make informed decisions, without paying a commission to anyone in the middle.

Every room on MeroSpace is admin-reviewed before it goes live. We check for accuracy, fair pricing, and real contact information so you can search with confidence.
