Who We Are

MeroSpace is a Kathmandu-born room rental platform built by people who understand the frustration of finding a decent room in the valley. We've all been there — calling brokers who never call back, visiting rooms that look nothing like the description, and paying fees just to see a listing.

We built MeroSpace to cut all of that out. Our platform connects room owners directly with tenants — no middlemen, no listing fees, no surprises. Just real rooms, real prices, and real contact information.

Whether you're a student moving to Kathmandu for college, a working professional looking for a flat near your office, or a family searching for a comfortable home in Lalitpur or Bhaktapur — MeroSpace is built for you.

Our Mission

The rental market in Nepal has long been dominated by word-of-mouth and unregulated brokers. Tenants overpay. Landlords struggle to find reliable tenants. And both sides waste enormous time in the process.

Our mission is simple: make renting transparent, fast, and fair. We verify listings, enforce quality standards, and give both landlords and tenants the tools they need to make informed decisions.

Every room on MeroSpace goes through an admin review before it goes live. We check for accuracy, fair pricing, and real contact information — so you can search with confidence.
